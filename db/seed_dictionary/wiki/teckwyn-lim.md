## The Author

Teckwyn Lim runs Resource Stewardship Consultants Sdn Bhd, is an honorary associate professor in environmental and geographical sciences at the University of Nottingham Malaysia, and is affiliated with Universiti Malaya’s Centre for Malaysian Indigenous Studies. He studies interactions between people and nature.
