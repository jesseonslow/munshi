## Biography

Dr Brian T. Urwin studies Malaysia’s colonial history and Britain’s colonial administrative service. He holds an MSc History from the University of Edinburgh and a PhD from the University of London’s Institute of Historical Research, where he is currently a research fellow. He became a member of MBRAS upon retiring from a career in industry in 2019.
