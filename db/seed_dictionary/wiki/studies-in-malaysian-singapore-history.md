# Studies in Malaysian & Singapore History

**SKU:** M42 | **Year:** 2010 | **Pages:** 208

## About

Almost every year history undergraduates at the honours level at the National University of Singapore (NUS) undertake research and write a thesis. This volume presents five outstanding graduation essays prepared during the 2008/2009 academic year selected and edited for publication under the Society’s Mubin Sheppard Memorial Fund.

## Contents

- Foreword by Dr Cheah Boon Kheng
- Introduction by Bruce Lockhart and Lim Tse Siang
  1. Relocated and Redefined: The History of Evicted Datuk Gongs in Singapore by Chiew Jing Wen
  1. Enabling the Singapore Story: Writing a History of Disability by Victor Zhuang Kuan Song
  1. The Proclamation of Independence Memorial in Malacca: History, Memory and Silences by Eisen Teo
  1. Under the Skin: Anxieties of the Domestic Realm in the Maria Hertogh Controversy by Christina Wu
  1. The Imperial Locomotive: A Study of the Railway System in British Malaya 1885-1942 by Lim Tse Siang
