# Report on the destruction of coco-nut palms by beetles

This entry catalogues a short entomological report by H. N. Ridley on beetle damage to coconut palms, published in No. 20 of the *Journal of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society* (JSBRAS), dated 1889 but issued after February 1890. The paper occupies eleven pages (pp. 1–11) and belongs to the Straits Branch series (Nos. 1–86, July 1878 to November 1922), which predates the Malayan Branch series (Vols. 1–20, 1923–47) indexed by C. A. Gibson-Hill in 1948.

## Summary

**Scope.** A single-author report on the destruction of coconut palms by beetles, presumably in the Straits Settlements. The paper is attributed to H. N. Ridley (Henry Nicholas Ridley, 1855–1957), who served as Director of the Singapore Botanic Gardens and was a leading authority on tropical palms and their pests.

**Organization.** The paper is a self-contained report of eleven pages' length, published as part of a serial journal issue rather than as a standalone monograph. No internal subdivisions are recorded in the available index materials.

**Usage.** The paper is indexed in the Wurtzburg Index (1927) to the Straits Branch Journals, published in *J.M.B.R.A.S.*, Vol. 5, pt 4 (p. 1). It does not appear in the Gibson-Hill Index (1948), which covers only the Malayan Branch series. No. 20 of the JSBRAS is recorded as having no surviving copies in the Society's stock (p. 64).

## Context

No. 20 of the JSBRAS carries the date 1889 but was in fact published after February 1890 (p. 64). The Straits Branch series was superseded by the Malayan Branch in 1923; the two series are indexed separately, the Straits Branch by Wurtzburg (1927) and the Malayan Branch by Gibson-Hill (1948) (p. 1). The Society holds no copies of No. 20, and the paper is therefore accessible only through the Wurtzburg Index or through institutional holdings listed in the Gibson-Hill front matter (pp. 4–7).
