# A journey over the main range from Perak to Pahang

This document is the Rules of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society, published as front matter in JSBRAS Vol. 68 (1915). It is an unattributed governance text setting out the constitutional framework of the Society.

## Summary

The Rules define the Society's officer structure (a President, three Vice Presidents resident in Singapore, Penang, and the Federated Malay States, an Honorary Secretary, Treasurer, Librarian, and four Councillors), all elected annually (p. 2). The Council, composed of the current year's officers, holds authority over administration, membership elections, publication of the Journal, library acquisitions, and by-law enforcement, meeting monthly with a quorum of three (p. 2).

The remaining sections govern General Meetings (annual meeting in February, quorum of eleven, casting vote for the Chairman), the publication of the Journal at least twice yearly with each member entitled to one free copy, and the procedure for amending the Rules, which requires written proposal to the Council, passage at a duly summoned General Meeting, and confirmation at a subsequent meeting (pp. 3–4).
