# Two Sea-Dayak legends. {With a note H.N. Ridley

This document records the proceedings of the Annual General Meeting of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society, held on 19 January 1904, chaired by Bishop Hose.

## Summary

The minutes record the confirmation of the previous meeting's minutes, the admission of a new subscriber (Lieut.-Colonel G. C. E. van Daalan), and the adoption of the Council's Annual Report and the Treasurer's audited accounts (p. 2). Officers for the ensuing year were elected, with Bishop Hose continuing as President and H. N. Ridley as Hon. Secretary (p. 3). The President noted that the Society had reached its twenty-fifth anniversary, having been founded on 4 November 1877 (p. 3).
