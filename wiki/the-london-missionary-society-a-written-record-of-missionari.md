# The London Missionary Society: a written record of missionaries and printing presses in the Straits Settlements 1815–1847

This is a brief errata notice issued by the editors of JMBRAS, correcting minor errors in Leona O'Sullivan's 1984 article on the London Missionary Society in the Straits Settlements.

## Summary

The notice lists three amendments to the original paper published in Vol. 57, Part 2 (December 1984): a credit line for the photograph of Rev. Robert Morrison (p. 63), a correction to a plate caption on p. 69 (from "8 parts Malacca 1829" to "21 Parts Malacca 1823"), and a spelling correction of "Abdullah" to "Abdallah" on p. 73 with a corresponding fix to footnote 46 on p. 100, plus a note on the romanization of "chuan."
