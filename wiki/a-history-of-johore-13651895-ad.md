# A history of Johore (1365–1895 A.D.

This is a fragment of the proceedings of the General Meeting of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society, held at the Exchange Rooms on 21st February 1883, recording attendance, member approvals, and the election of officers for 1883.

## Summary

The document records the attendance of members including Col. S. Dunlop, N. B. Dennys, Hugo Dennys, Bennett Pell, and others, followed by the approval of six gentlemen provisionally elected by the Council since the previous annual meeting (p. 2–3). The Honorary Secretary read the Annual Report of the Council for 1882, and the Honorary Treasurer read his report. Officers for 1883 were elected by ballot, with Mr. W. E. Maxwell elected Honorary Secretary, though he had not yet returned to the Colony; Mr. Skinner was authorised to continue in the role in the interim (p. 3).
