# Jawi literature in Patani: the maintenance of an Islamic tradition

Annual Report of the Straits Branch Royal Asiatic Society for 1911, authored by R. Hanitsch (Acting Hon. Secretary) and Walter Makepeace (Honorary Treasurer), published in the *Journal of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society*, No. 61 (June 1912), pp. viii–x.

## Summary

The report records a year of unusual activity, including the publication of four numbers of the Journal (Nos. 57–60) and the long-delayed publication of a new Map of the Malay Peninsula, which cost a total of $5,457.71 to produce and of which 358 copies had been sold by the end of December (p. viii). Eighteen new members were elected during the year, and the deaths of two members—the Hon'ble W. D. Barnes and Mr. W. Nanson, a member since 1889—were recorded (pp. viii–ix).

The report also notes the resignation of Mr. H. N. Ridley, C.M.G., F.R.S., as Secretary in October 1911 in view of his approaching retirement; Ridley had served as Secretary for most of the Society's existence from 1890 to 1911 (p. ix). The accompanying Honorary Treasurer's Account for 1911, audited by Walter Makepeace on 24 January 1912, details receipts of $8,209.30 and payments of $3,738.74, with the balance carried forward in fixed and current deposits at the Mercantile and Chartered Banks (p. x).
