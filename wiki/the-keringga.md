# The keringga

This brief note by W. D. records the capture and identification of a pair of *Eudromias veredus* (Gould) in Singapore, published in JSBRAS Vol. 23 (1891).

## Summary

The author describes how a native bird-catcher, using hair nooses on a race course, captured a pair of plovers (male and female) unfamiliar to him. They were purchased for 10 cents and subsequently mounted. The birds are identified as *Eudromias veredus* Gould, originally described in the Proceedings of the Zoological Society (1848, p. 38). The species is noted as rare in this region, having been recorded only once from the Andamans and once from the Sunderbunds, though it occurs not unfrequently in Java and islands farther east (p. 148).

The note closes with a brief additional observation of a flight of approximately 20 birds of the rare tern *Sterna leucoptera* at Penang in May (p. 148).
