# Currency, Negri Sembilan

A brief note by A. H. (with R. N. B.) reporting two artifacts from Perak that provide evidence of Siamese presence in the state's tin-mining areas (p. 356).

## Summary

The author describes a *Kachit* (betel-nut cutter) recovered from a depth of about six feet in a hill-mine near Ipoh, Kinta, which differs from the local Malay form by having two corrugated crushing surfaces rather than a cutting blade—a pattern reported as common in Siam (p. 356). A more significant find is a semi-elliptical tin ingot of about two *kati*, discovered in mine workings near Kwala Dipang, Kampar, bearing an inscription in Siamese characters that the author was unable to decipher locally (p. 357). Both specimens were sent to the Perak Museum at Thaipeng for further study.
