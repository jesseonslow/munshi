# Further work on Indian sites in Malaya

This entry documents a research article by D. C. Wales and H. G. Q. Wales on ancient Indian archaeological sites in the Malay Peninsula, published in the first part of Volume 20 of the *Journal of the Malayan Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society* in 1947 (pp. 1–11). It constitutes a sequel to Wales's earlier 1940 study and is indexed under "Archaeology & Prehistory" and "Indian Colonisation & Influence" in the Gibson-Hill Index (1948), with cross-references to the states of Kedah and Perak and to the Srivijaya question (pp. 38, 45–46, 53, 56).

## Summary

**Scope.** The paper surveys and discusses ancient Indian sites in Malaya, with particular attention to material in Kedah and in the Kuala Selinsing area of Perak. It engages with the broader question of early Indian colonisation and influence in the region, including its relationship to the Srivijaya polity (p. 45). The work is a continuation of Wales (1940), which is likewise indexed under the same headings (p. 38).

**Organization.** The article is a single continuous piece of eleven pages, not divided into separately titled sections. In the Gibson-Hill Subject Index it is cited under multiple headings: "Indian Sites, Ancient" (p. 38); "Ancient Sites in Malaya" under "Indian Colonisation & Influence" (p. 45); "Prehistory & Indian Colonisation" under "Kedah" (p. 46); "Indian Colonisation, Early in" under "Perak" (p. 53); and as a cross-reference under "Srivijaya" (p. 56).

**Usage.** Researchers seeking the full bibliographic details (volume, part, page range) should consult Part 1 of the Gibson-Hill Index, where papers are listed chronologically under the authors' names with complete references (p. 1). The Subject Index supplies only the author's name and year of publication, as is standard for that section (p. 37).

## Context

The paper is situated within a small body of related work indexed in the same volume: Braddell (1934, 1935, 1941, 1947) on the "Pallava Seal" and general prehistory; Evans (1936) on human remains from rock shelters; Sastri (1936) on the Pallava Seal; and Winstedt (1941) on the Perak site of the Sailendra Empire (pp. 38, 45, 56). The Gibson-Hill Index notes that Braddell's 1935 general summary of ancient times in the Malay Peninsula and the Straits of Malacca is analysed in Appendix 4 of that index (p. 38, n. 1), providing a further point of access for readers working on the same subject matter.
