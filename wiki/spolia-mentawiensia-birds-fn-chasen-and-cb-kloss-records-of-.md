# Spolia mentawiensia: Birds. F.N. Chasen and C.B. Kloss. Records of the Raffles Museum, No. 25

This brief notice by [F.N. Chasen](./fn-chasen.md) and [C.B. Kloss](./cb-kloss.md) (1926) reports on two contributions to the "Spolia Mentawiensia" series (Records of the Raffles Museum, No. 25), covering the birds and the [reptiles](./reptiles.md) and amphibians collected during the [Raffles](./stamford-raffles.md) Museum's expedition to the [Mentawi Islands](./mentawi-islands.md), West [Sumatra](./sumatra.md).

The birds section, reviewed by C.B.K., notes that the authors consider little remains for future ornithological discovery on the Mentawis except perhaps among migrants and shore-birds, and that their "List of Mentawi Birds" is not regarded as final. The paper is illustrated by a map and a coloured plate of a new subspecies of Serpent-eagle, *Spilornis elgini sipora* (p. 263). The reptiles and amphibians section, by Malcolm A. Smith, lists species newly recorded from the islands, including [lizards](./lizards.md), [snakes](./snakes.md), and amphibians from Siberut and Sipora, and describes two new forms: *Boiga nigriceps brevicauda* and *Calamaria klossi*. Smith concludes that the herpetofauna of the West Sumatran chain is distinctly Sumatran, though a few species of peculiar distribution are known from the [islands](./islands.md) but not yet from the larger island (pp. 263–264).
