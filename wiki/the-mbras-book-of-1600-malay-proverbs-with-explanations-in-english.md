# The MBRAS book of 1,600 Malay Proverbs with Explanations in English

**SKU:** M22 | **Year:** 1992 | **Pages:** 195

## About

Malay was once a language used by people who lived in rural areas, along riverbanks, beside rice fields, and in maritime communities along the coast of the peninsula. The Malay Peninsula developed strong oral [traditions](./traditions.md), including proverbs or proverbial sayings that captured the received wisdom of its communities, and the [Malay proverbs](./malay-proverbs.md) assembled in this book provide a vivid portrait of village life, with many references to interactions with forests, [plants](./plants.md), birds, wildlife, and [fish](./fish.md). These proverbs, which were in many cases first written down by pioneer British civil servants, such as Sir [Hugh Clifford](./sir-hugh-charles-clifford.md), Sir William Maxwell, and [R. J. Wilkinson](./richard-james-wilkinson.md), help preserve the richness of the [Malay](./malay.md) language and cultural elements that might otherwise have been lost.

## The Author

Edward Shaw [Hose, C](./c-hose.md).M.G. (1871-1946) was the son of the Right Rev. George Frederick Hose, former Bishop of [Singapore](./singapore.md) and founder president of the [MBRAS](./malaysian-branch-of-the-royal-asiatic-society.md). Hose was born on 25 November 1871 and entered the Civil Service of the Federated Malay States (F.M.S) in 1891 as a Junior Officer in Perak. He served with distinction in several capacities in the F.M.S., mostly in [Perak](./perak.md), before being appointed Colonial Secretary in 1924 after having received a C.M.G. in 1923. This particular appointment was due mostly to the exceptional ability he displayed when he acted as Chief Secretary of the [F.M.S.](./federated-malay-states.md) during a particularly difficult period in 1923. Hose retired in the early 1930s. Like his father, he was a religious man and was deeply connected throughout his life with church affairs.
