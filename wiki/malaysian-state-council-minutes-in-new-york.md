# Malaysian State Council minutes in New York

This is a short note by W.R. Roff documenting the discovery of Malaysian State Council minutes held in New York, published in the *Journal of the Malaysian Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society* (Vol. 42, No. 2, 1969).

The note identifies a collection of Malaysian State Council minutes held in New York, providing details of their provenance and content. Roff's brief account serves as a bibliographic pointer for researchers seeking primary source material on Malaysian state-level governance, noting the location and nature of the archival holdings (p. 213).
