# Nesting of Draco fimbriatus

This is a brief note by H. N. Ridley describing an observation of a wasp attacking a leaf-mining caterpillar, published in the *Journal of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society*, Vol. 44 (1905), p. 228.

## Summary

Ridley records the observation of a common wasp species that makes small nests on the underside of leaves, identifiable by its dark red thorax and black abdomen bearing a single yellow ring. The note highlights the peculiarity that this wasp preyed on a leaf-mining caterpillar and followed its burrow beneath the epidermis, a behaviour noted as unusual even among the smaller wasps known to prey on caterpillars (p. 228).
