# New Chalcid parasites from Malaya

This is a short taxonomic note by A.A. Girault describing new Chalcid parasites from Malaya, based on specimens collected by C.F. Baker, with types deposited in the Raffles Museum, Singapore (p. 165).

## Summary

Girault names five taxa from a collection sent by Baker, noting that the material showed marked affinities to Australian forms and that novel genera were rare (p. 165). The described taxa comprise a new variety (*Leptomastix guttatipennis io* var. nov.), a new species (*Leptomastix penangi* sp. nov.), two new genera (*Rhynchentedon* in Entedonini and *Lutheria* in Encyrtidae), and a further new species (*Systolomorphella lyra* sp. nov.) (pp. 165–167). Each entry provides brief morphological diagnoses and locality data (Singapore or Penang), with the new genera receiving more extended descriptions of head, thoracic, and abdominal characters.
