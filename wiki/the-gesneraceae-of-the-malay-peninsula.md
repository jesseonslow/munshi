# The Gesneraceae of the Malay Peninsula

This is a brief note by H. N. Ridley on the nesting habits of a bird species, published in the Short Notes section of JSBRAS Vol. 44 (1905).

## Summary

Ridley observes that the bird in question must nest frequently given its commonness, and notes having often seen it making holes in trees as if to nest. He describes one individual that appeared to roost regularly in a hole it had made in a dead bough in the Gardens, where it could be seen every evening at the mouth of the hole, yet despite it being the breeding season, he found neither eggs nor young birds in the nest (p. 227).
