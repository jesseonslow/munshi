# Kedah natural history notes

This note by A. W. Hamilton records Malay names and brief natural history observations for a selection of birds and fish encountered in Kedah, published in the Journal of the MBRAS in 1930 (p. 330).

## Summary

Hamilton provides corrected or verified Malay names for approximately fifteen species, including the scarlet-backed Flower-pecker (*Sepah Peteri*), the Fantail Flycatcher (*Chenchala* or *Murai Gila*), the Purple Heron (*Seriap*), and the Malayan Banded Crake (*Sintar Api*), alongside a few mammals and freshwater fish (p. 330–333). Several entries include short ethnographic remarks: the heron's former abundance and its nesting in *perepok* reed beds, with royalty collecting eggs on elephant-backs; the banded crake's capture in rotan basket traps set in converging twig fences; and the use of serow meat and spittle as a healing unguent (p. 331–332). The note closes with a description of the Malay practice of pitting *ikan pelaga* (fighting fish of the genus *Betta*) against one another in glass jars, with bets placed on the outcome (p. 332–333).
