# Proposed English–Malay dictionary

This notice, published in the *[Journal of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society](./journal-of-the-malaysian-branch-of-the-royal-asiatic-society.md)* (Vol. 13, 1884), announces the proposed publication of an English–[Malay](./malay.md) [dictionary](./dialects-and-dictionaries.md) by H. C. Klinkert, to be issued by E. J. Brill of Leyden.

## Summary

The dictionary is to be adapted from Klinkert's Dutch–Malay dictionary then in press, and its publication is contingent on securing sufficient subscribers. The Malay transcription follows the [Straits Settlements](./straits-settlements.md) dialect as used by Maxwell and Keasberry, and the English portion will be revised by a native Englishman. The work is expected to run to approximately 1,000–1,200 pages, with a subscription price of £1 5s. bound (p. 274). A companion Malay–English volume is also in preparation. A subscription list was opened at the [Raffles](./stamford-raffles.md) Library, [Singapore](./singapore.md), where a specimen sheet could be inspected (p. 274).
