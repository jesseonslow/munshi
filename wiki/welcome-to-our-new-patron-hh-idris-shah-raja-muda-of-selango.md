# Welcome to our new patron (HH Idris Shah Raja Muda of Selangor)

This is a brief correction note by C. O. Blagden clarifying diacritical distinctions in his earlier transcription of the Kota Kapur Inscription, published in the Journal of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society, No. 65 (1913).

## Summary

Blagden explains that the printer of his earlier note (No. 64) lacked suitable type to distinguish between ordinary *n*, *s*, *h* and their dotted variants. He clarifies that words ending in *h* in the inscription actually use the Indian *visarga* symbol, that dotted *s* (representing *sh*) appears in *dosa* (i.e. *dosha*), and that the critical distinction is between simple *n* and dotted *n* (the velar nasal, written *ng* in Romanized Malay). He then re-lists the relevant words — *urang*, *di dalangna*, *pulang*, *mulang*, *mang-*, *mang-i*, *mangujari*, *ding*, *dngan*, *jangan*, *yang*, *iyang*, *grang* — in corrected spelling to show their closer correspondence with modern Malay equivalents (p. 2).
