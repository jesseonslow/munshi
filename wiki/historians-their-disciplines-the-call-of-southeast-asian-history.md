# Historians & Their Disciplines: The Call of Southeast Asian History

**SKU:** M40 | **Year:** 2007 | **Pages:** 202

## About

In this unique compilation of personal essays by a group of eminent historians specializing in [Southeast Asia](./southeast-asia.md), [Wang Gungwu](./wang-gungwu.md), John [Gullick](./john-michael-gullick.md), [Cheah Boon Kheng](./cheah-boon-kheng.md), Ruth McVey, and the editor comment on the reasons behind their choice of specialization, and how they pursued their research interests, academic careers, and writing on their chosen topics. The also comment on the development of this field of specialization and the future that awaits it. These intimate and highly readable accounts, mainly semiautobiographical in nature, are illuminating and reveal many "trade secrets". This is a companion volume to [New Perspectives and Research on Malaysian History](./new-perspectives-and-research-on-malaysian-history.md), a collection of essays on Malaysian historiography also published by the [MBRAS](./malaysian-branch-of-the-royal-asiatic-society.md).
