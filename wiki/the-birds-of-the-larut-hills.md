# The birds of the Larut Hills

This one-page fragment, attributed to A. L. Butler in JSBRAS Vol. 34 (1900), p. 99, contains a botanical description of a new orchid species rather than ornithological content matching its title.

## Summary

The text describes *Tapeinocheilus Koordersiana* n. sp., a tall (25-foot) orchid from Celebes, detailing its leaves, spike, bracts, calyx, corolla, lip, anther, and capsule. The author notes its affinity with Miquel's *T. pungens* but distinguishes it by larger flowers and pubescent bracts, and dedicates the name to Dr. Koorders for his plant collections in Celebes (p. 99). A brief preceding sentence references *Zebrinus* of gardens and its lack of relationship to the genus *Costus*.

## Context

The content of this page is entirely botanical and bears no relation to the title "The Birds of the Larut Hills." This appears to be a mis-scanned or mis-attributed page from a different article in the same volume, or a pagination error in the digitised record. The actual ornithological text by Butler is not present in this fragment.
