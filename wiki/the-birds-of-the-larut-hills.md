# The birds of the Larut Hills

This document, catalogued as "The Birds of the Larut Hills" by A.L. Butler (JSBRAS Vol. 34, 1900, p. 99), contains a botanical description rather than ornithological content.

## Summary

The page as scanned presents a description of a new orchid species, *Tapeinocheilus Koordersiana* n. sp., from Celebes, allied to Miquel's *T. pungens* but distinguished by larger flowers and pubescent bracts (p. 99). The species is named in honour of Dr. Koorders, who made fine plant collections in Celebes. The text also references a garden plant called *Zebrinus*, noted as having no relationship with the genus *Costus* (p. 99).

The content on this page does not correspond to the title "The Birds of the Larut Hills" as recorded in the journal's table of contents, suggesting a possible mis-scanning or pagination error in the digitised volume.
