# A new squirrel from Tenggol Island, east coast of the Malay Peninsula. Records of the Raffles Museum, No. 23

C. Boden Kloss describes a new subspecies of coconut squirrel, *Sciurus notatus guillemardi*, collected from Pulau Tenggol on the east coast of the Malay Peninsula (p. 260).

## Summary

The new subspecies is named in honour of Sir Laurence Guillemard. It is a small squirrel of the *vittatus* section of *notatus*, approximately the size of *S. scotti* from Little Redang and *S. famulus* from Pulau Dayang, but distinguished by its darker rufous underparts and tail tip, a richer buff in the pale elements of the upper parts, and a broader lateral stripe (p. 260). Cranially it resembles *S. famulus* in rostrum length and in the posterior extent of the nasals relative to the premaxillaries (p. 260).

The type is an adult female collected by Kloss on 18 June 1926; three additional adult specimens (two males, one female) from the same locality and date were examined. Kloss notes that one specimen shows reduced dark lateral stripes obscured with rufous, suggesting a possible parallel with colour tendencies observed in races from the more northern Trengganu islands (p. 260). The note includes collector's measurements and a table of skull dimensions for all four specimens (pp. 260–261).
