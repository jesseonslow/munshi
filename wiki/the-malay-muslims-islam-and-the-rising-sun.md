# The Malay Muslims, Islam and the Rising Sun

**SKU:** M34 | **Year:** 2003 | **Pages:** 288

## About

This study, based mainly on local documentary materials in the National [Archives](./archives.md) of [Malaysia](./malaysia.md) and its various branches, focuses on Malay-Muslim social life and the religious bureaucracy in selected Malay states such as [Johore](./johor.md) during the Japanese Occupation of [Malaya](./malaya.md) between 1941 and 1945. The study also looks at the memories of Malay-Muslims about [various](./various.md) aspects of the occupation and the emotive issue of resistance and collaboration within this society. In presenting the Japanese policy towards Islam and the Malay-Muslims, the author shows that it was mainly a propaganda machinery to win a war in which neither [Islam](./islam.md) nor [Malay](./malay.md)-Muslims had any vested interest.

## The Author

Datuk Professor Dr [Abu Talib Ahmad](./abu-talib-ahmad.md) is Dean of the School of Humanities at Universiti Sains Malaysia and teaches modern Southeast Asian history, Japanese history and the Japanese Occupation in [Southeast Asia](./southeast-asia.md). His research and publication interests include modern [Burma](./burma.md), Japanese history, the Japanese Occupation of Malaya and Malaya's social history.
