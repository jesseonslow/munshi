# Malay Sufism as illustrated in an anonymous collection of 17th century tracts

These are the minutes of the Annual General Meeting of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society, held at the Raffles Museum, Singapore, on 31 January 1896 and adjourned to 4 February 1896, published in 1897.

## Summary

The first session records the sitting officers (President: the Bishop of Singapore; Vice Presidents: W. R. Collyer and Daniel Logan; Honorary Secretary: C. O. Blagden; Honorary Treasurer: J. O. Anthonisz) and a vote of thanks to retiring officers, with special mention of R. J. Wilkinson and the Rev. G. M. Reith (p. xvi). A sub-committee of H. H. Eschke and W. G. St. Clair was appointed to confer with the new Council on proposed alterations to the results, and the question of reducing the quorum for ordinary General Meetings was referred to the same committee (p. xvi).

The adjourned session of 4 February 1896 records an amendment to Rule 26 requiring that any proposed amendment to the Rules be stated in the Notice summoning the Meeting, and a unanimous resolution that ladies were eligible for membership of the Society, consistent with the practice of the Royal Asiatic Society of Great Britain and Ireland (p. xviii).
