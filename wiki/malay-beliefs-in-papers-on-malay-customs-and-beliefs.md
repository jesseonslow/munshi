# Malay beliefs. {In Papers on Malay customs and beliefs

This document records the proceedings of the Annual General Meeting of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society, held at the Raffles Museum, Singapore, on 31 January 1896 and adjourned to 4 February 1896.

## Summary

The minutes document the election of officers (including the Bishop of Singapore as President), a vote of thanks to retiring officers with special mention of R. J. Wilkinson and the Rev. G. M. Reith, and the appointment of a sub-committee to consider proposed alterations to the Society's rules and the quorum requirement (p. 17). At the adjourned meeting, an amendment to Rule 26 was adopted requiring that proposed rule changes be stated in the notice summoning the meeting, and it was unanimously resolved that ladies were eligible for membership, consistent with the practice of the Royal Asiatic Society of Great Britain and Ireland (p. 18).
