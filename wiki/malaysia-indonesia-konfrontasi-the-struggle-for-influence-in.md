# Malaysia-Indonesia Konfrontasi : the struggle for influence in the Middle East. I. Karuppannan and Shakila Yacob

This article by Ilango Karuppannan and Shakila Yacob, published in JMBRAS Vol. 93 (1), 2020, examines the Malaysia-Indonesia Konfrontasi (1963–1966) through the lens of broader Cold War power competition, specifically framing the dispute as part of a "struggle for influence in the Middle East."

## Summary

The reference list indicates the article draws on a wide range of primary and secondary sources spanning British, American, Australian, and New Zealand diplomatic records, as well as Malaysian archival materials held at Arkib Negara Malaysia (transferred from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in 2014). The bibliography encompasses the full arc of the Konfrontasi — from its genesis in the context of the formation of Malaysia, through the involvement of external powers, to its resolution following the 1965–66 political upheaval in Indonesia (pp. 87–89).

The inclusion of sources on the Bandung Conference, Sino-Indonesian dual nationality, and the "Asian Solution" approach, alongside works on the September 30 Movement and its aftermath, suggests the authors situate the Konfrontasi within a wider geopolitical framework linking Southeast Asian and Middle Eastern Cold War dynamics. The article appears to be a substantial study (pp. 67–90) rather than a brief note, though only the front matter and references were available for this archival record.
