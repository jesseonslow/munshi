# British policy in the Malay Peninsula and Archipelago, 1824–1871

This document records the proceedings of the Annual General Meeting of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society, held at the Raffles Museum, Singapore, on 31 January 1896, with an adjourned session on 4 February 1896.

## Summary

The meeting lists the Branch's officers (President: the Bishop of Singapore; Vice Presidents: W. R. Collyer and Daniel Logan; Honorary Secretary: C. O. Blagden; Honorary Treasurer: J. O. Anthonisz) and councillors (p. 3). A vote of thanks was proposed to retiring officers, with special reference to R. J. Wilkinson and the Rev. G. M. Reith, and a sub-committee (H. H. Eschke and W. G. St. Clair) was appointed to consider altering election results and reducing the quorum for ordinary General Meetings (p. 3).

The adjourned meeting of 4 February 1896 recorded an amendment to Rule 26 requiring that any proposed amendment to the Rules be stated in the Notice summoning the Meeting, and a unanimous resolution that ladies were eligible for membership, aligning the Straits Branch with the practice of the Royal Asiatic Society of Great Britain and Ireland (p. 5).
