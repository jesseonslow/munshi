# Malay equivalents for military terms

This is a brief note by Capt. N. M. Hashim providing Malay equivalents for British military ranks and organizational designations, published in the *Journal of the Malayan Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society* (1924).

## Summary

The note presents a list of Malay terms corresponding to British military ranks and unit designations, including Lance Corporal (*Alang Dekar*), Private (*Gagah*), Commander-in-Chief (*Imam Prang*), Colonel-in-Chief (*Panglima Atas*), Staff Officer (*Pegawai Dalam*), Warrant Officer (*Perwira*), and various unit terms such as Battalion (*Ketumbakan Agong*), Company (*Ketumbakan*), and Platoon (*Sayap*) (p. 84). The document as digitized also includes the concluding portion of a preceding Malay vocabulary list by G. Beresford Stooke on page 83, which covers entries from "Tembang" through "Wongmeni."
