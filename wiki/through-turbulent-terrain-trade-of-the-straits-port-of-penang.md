# Through Turbulent Terrain: Trade of the Straits Port of Penang

**SKU:** M50 | **Year:** 2018 | **Pages:** 273

## About

In 1786 the British [East India Company](./east-india-company.md) established a port on Penang that became a major commercial centre despite minimal support from [India](./india.md) and London, and Dutch machinations in [Sumatra](./sumatra.md). Contrary to the conventional view that [Singapore](./singapore.md) by-passed Penang after 1819, Penang remained an important centre of entrepôt trade throughout the colonial period and served as major conduit for the [Malaya](./malaya.md)’s tin and rubber trade. Through Turbulent Terrain uses a broad range of sources to trace the changing fortunes of [Penang](./penang.md)’s regional trade.

This volume benefits from the generous sponsorship by Think City Sdn Bhd, an urban regeneration and solutions provider with offices in Penang, [Kuala Lumpur](./kuala-lumpur.md) and [Johor](./johor.md) Bahru.

## The Authors

[Loh Wei Leng](./loh-wei-leng.md) is a former lecturer at the History Department of the University of Malaya and an [MBRAS](./malaysian-branch-of-the-royal-asiatic-society.md) Vice-President. [Jeffery Seow](./jeffery-seow.md) is her co-researcher with whom she worked on this book.
