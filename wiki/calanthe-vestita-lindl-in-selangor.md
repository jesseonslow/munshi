# Calanthe vestita Lindl. in Selangor

This short note by H. N. Ridley records the discovery of *Calanthe vestita* Lindl. on the limestone rocks at the Kuala Lumpur caves (Gua Batu), Selangor, in December 1896.

## Summary

Ridley notes that *C. vestita* has a remarkable distribution, being known from Tenasserim and Borneo, and that its occurrence in the intermediate region—particularly on limestone—was to be expected. The plant was found as a single specimen in a crevice in a tree on the upper limestone rocks at Gua Batu, in perfect flower and of a fine form. He observes that the upper flora of these rocks differs markedly from that of the lower cliffs and resembles the limestone flora of the Lankawi islands, where *C. vestita* is replaced by *C. rubens* Ridl. (p. 311).

Ridley frames this discovery as a connecting link between the two widely separated regions of Tenasserim and Borneo, and adds a parallel example: *Dendrobium heterocarpum* Wall. (*D. aureum* Lindl.), known from India, Java, and the Philippines, had recently been found by A. B. Stephens on the Thaiping hills in Perak. (p. 312)
