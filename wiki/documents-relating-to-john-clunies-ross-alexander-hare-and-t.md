# Documents relating to John Clunies Ross, Alexander Hare, and the establishment of the colony on the Cocos-Keeling Islands

The Rules of the Straits Asiatic Society, published as front matter in the *Journal of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society* (January 1894, No. 25, pp. xxi–xxiv), set out the constitutional framework governing the society's membership, officers, council, meetings, publications, and amendment procedures.

## Summary

The document comprises twenty-six rules organised into eight sections covering membership categories (including Honorary Members proposed by the Council, p. xxii), the composition and duties of the Officers and Council (pp. xxii–xxiii), the conduct of Annual and General Meetings with quorum requirements (p. xxiii), the publication of a semi-annual Journal with distribution provisions for members and authors (pp. xxiii–xxiv), the sanctioning of popular lectures, and the procedure for amending the rules themselves (p. xxiv). The Council is given broad administrative authority over the society's property, library, and editorial decisions, subject to confirmation by general meeting for by-laws (p. xxiii).
