# Early Singapore and the inception of a British administrative tradition in the Straits Settlements (1819–32

This article by J. Kathirithamby-Wells, published in the *Journal of the Malaysian Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society* (Vol. 42, No. 2, 1969, pp. 48–73), examines the formative period of British administration in Singapore and the Straits Settlements between 1819 and 1832.

## Summary

The provided source text consists solely of the article's front matter (title, author, and publication details) and does not include the body of the article. Based on the title, the piece addresses the establishment of British administrative structures in early colonial Singapore and their role in shaping the broader administrative tradition of the Straits Settlements during the first thirteen years of British presence on the island.
