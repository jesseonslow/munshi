# List of publications. {J.M. Gullick

This is a bibliographic list of publications by John M. Gullick, compiled for JMBRAS Vol. 72 (Issue 2), 1999 (pp. 183–186).

## Summary

The document catalogues Gullick's published output across four categories: books (14 titles, 1956–1995), articles in JMBRAS and MBRAS Monographs (31 items, 1949–1998), articles and reviews in other journals (16 items), and miscellaneous contributions including introductions to reprints, encyclopedia entries, and memoirs (p. 183–186). The books span Malay political history, Kuala Lumpur's development, and edited anthologies of European travel writing in South-East Asia. The JMBRAS/Monograph articles cover a wide range of topics from early Selangor and Kedah history to the Malay press, adat law, and the Society's own institutional history (p. 183–184). The list also records forthcoming works at the time of publication, including a full *History of Kuala Lumpur 1857–1939* as an MBRAS Monograph and entries for the *New Dictionary of National Biography* (p. 184, 186).
