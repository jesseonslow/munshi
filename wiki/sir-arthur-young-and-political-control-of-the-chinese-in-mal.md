# Sir Arthur Young and political control of the Chinese in Malaya and the Straits Settlements

This is a joint obituary for Bishop George Frederick Hose (1838–1908), first Bishop of Singapore and third Bishop of Sarawak, written by R. N. Bland and H. N. Ridley and published in the *Journal of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society*, No. 57 (1911), pp. 1–4.

## Summary

Bland's portion (pp. 1–3) traces Hose's career from his ordination in 1863 through his chaplaincies in Malacca and Singapore to his consecration as Bishop on Ascension Day 1881, and his retirement in 1908 after 40 years in the Far East. It notes that his jurisdiction encompassed the Straits Settlements, Java, Labuan and North Borneo, and that the division of the diocese he had urged was subsequently carried out, making him both the first and last Bishop of Singapore and Sarawak combined. Bland also records Hose's founding of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society in November 1877 and his service as its first President.

Ridley's contribution (pp. 3–4) focuses on Hose's botanical work, particularly his extensive fern collections from Borneo and the Malay Peninsula, several species of which bear his name (e.g., *Trichomanes Hosei*, *Davallia Hosei*). He also notes Hose's introduction of the Para rubber tree to Sarawak from Singapore seed, his cultivation of *Hosea* and *Pinanga arundinacea*, and the rediscovery of *Crinum Northianum* in Sarawak, from which living bulbs were sent to the Singapore Botanic Gardens for wider distribution.
