# Inscriptions in St. Paul’s Church, Malacca

This is a short botanical note by A. L. Butler describing new species of Eastern gingers (Zingiberaceae), published in the Journal of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society, Vol. 34 (1900).

## Summary

The text presents descriptions of three new species: *Alpinia Celebica* n. sp., a tall herb from Celebes with a deeply cleft lip allied to *A. Fraseriana* of Borneo; *Anomum terminale* n. sp., from the Bismarck Archipelago (Micholitz collection), notable for its terminal spike and red bracts, which Butler found difficult to refer to any existing genus (p. 3); and *Tapeinocheilus Koordersiana* n. sp., a 25-foot-tall plant from Celebes allied to Miquel's *T. pungens* but with larger flowers and pubescent bracts, named in honour of Dr. Koorders (p. 4). The opening fragment also contains a brief description of a *Rhacophorus* flying frog specimen, apparently from a separate article on the same pages (p. 2).
