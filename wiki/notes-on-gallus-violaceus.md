# Notes on Gallus violaceus

This document, catalogued as "Notes on Gallus violaceus" by H. J. Kelsall (JSBRAS Vol. 25, 1894, p. 173), contains a textual fragment that does not correspond to the stated title or author. The surviving text is the concluding portion of a note signed "H. N. R." describing a spider species.

## Summary

The fragment records an observation made during a visit to Gunong Panti in Johor, where the author encountered a spider of the genus *Ornithoscatoides* engaged in sucking the juice of a captured red bug. The spider had left its web—a thin circular white film on a wild plantain leaf—to devour its prey. The author compares the specimen with Forbes' *Ornithoscatoides decipiens* (collected in Java and Sumatra), noting differences in body colouring and several structural points, and concludes it is probably a distinct species (p. 173).

The note references the well-known deimatic mimicry behaviour of this genus, in which the spider spins a web on a leaf to simulate bird droppings and then lies on its back, waiting for an incautious butterfly to alight before seizing it (p. 173).
