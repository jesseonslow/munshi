# Notes on Gallus violaceus

This is a brief occasional note (p. 173) signed "H. N. R." describing a spider of the genus *Ornithoscatoides* encountered on Gunong Panti, Johor, and comparing it to Forbes' *O. decipiens* from Java and Sumatra.

## Summary

The note records the author's observation of a spider spinning a thin circular white film on a leaf of a wild plantain, mimicking bird droppings to attract prey. The specimen was found in the act of feeding on a captured red bug. The author compares it to *Ornithoscatoides decipiens*, described by H. O. Forbes and the Rev. O. Pickard Cambridge in *Wanderings of a Naturalist*, and concludes that differences in body colouring and structural points indicate the Gunong Panti specimen is probably a distinct species (p. 173).
