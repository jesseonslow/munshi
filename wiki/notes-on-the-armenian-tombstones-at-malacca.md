# Notes on the Armenian tombstones at Malacca

This is a short note by J. V. Mills on the Armenian tombstones at Malacca, published in the *Journal of the Malayan Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society*, Vol. 14, No. 3 (1936), pp. 264–271.

## Summary

The note documents and discusses the Armenian tombstones found at Malacca, contributing to the epigraphic and historical record of the Armenian community that had a presence in the Straits Settlements. The piece spans eight pages and is presented as a brief scholarly note rather than a full-length article.
