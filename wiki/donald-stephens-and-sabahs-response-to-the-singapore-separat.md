# Donald Stephens and Sabah’s Response to the Singapore Separation

## Abstract

The internal dynamics of the Federation of Malaysia changed with the departure of Singapore on 9 August 1965, leaving political leaders in Sabah uncertain about the future. On August 23rd, *The Sabah Times* published an editorial denying that Sabah had any interest in seceding from the federation. The editorial stated the official position of Donald Stephens, a prominent Sabah politician and owner of *The Sabah Times*, but the diplomatic correspondence of several British Commonwealth countries shows that it did not reflect Stephens’ true feelings. This article discusses events during the 14-day period between Singapore’s separation from Malaysia and publication of the editorial. It shows that Sabah’s leaders, and Stephens in particular, seriously contemplated leaving the federation but were dissuaded by a lack of support from Australia, New Zealand, and the United Kingdom, and by a strong stance of the government in Kuala Lumpur.
