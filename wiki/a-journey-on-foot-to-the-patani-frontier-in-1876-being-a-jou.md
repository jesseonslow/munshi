# A journey on foot to the Patani frontier in 1876; being a journal kept an expedition undertaken to capture Datoh Maharaja Lela of Perak

This is a brief errata notice correcting three textual errors in an article on the probable origin of the hill tribes of Formosa, published in JSBRAS Vol. 9 (1882). The author of the errata is not named in the source.

## Summary

The notice provides three corrections to the article "A Few Ideas on the Probable Origin of the Hill Tribes of Formosa" (Journal No. 9). On page 71, line 11, the word "then" is to be deleted; on line 13, the phrase "were in earlier times" is to be inserted after the word "group." On page 72, line 33, the name "Mr. A. U. Bain" is corrected to "Mr. A. N. Bain" (p. 2).
