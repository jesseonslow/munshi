# A Malay legal miscellany

This document is the constitutional Rules of the Straits Asiatic Society, published in the *Journal of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society* (January 1894, pp. xxi–xxiv). It is a front-matter text with no individual author.

## Summary

The Rules establish the governance framework of the Straits Asiatic Society, covering membership categories (Ordinary and Honorary), the composition and duties of the Council, the election of Officers (President, two Vice-Presidents, Honorary Secretary and Librarian, Honorary Treasurer, and five Councillors), and the conduct of meetings (p. xxii). Provisions for the Society's Journal specify semi-annual publication under Council supervision, with each member entitled to one copy and authors receiving twenty-four copies of their papers (p. xxiii). The Rules also provide for occasional Popular Lectures on literary or scientific subjects and a procedure for amending the Rules by written proposal to the Council followed by a report from a joint Committee of Resident Members (p. xxiv).
