# Early Muslim traders in South-East Asia

This document records the proceedings of the Annual General Meeting of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society, held at the Raffles Museum, Singapore, on 31 January 1896, and its adjourned session on 4 February 1896.

## Summary

The minutes list the Society's officers (President: the Bishop of Singapore; Vice Presidents: W. R. Collyer and Daniel Logan; Honorary Secretary: C. O. Blagden; Honorary Treasurer: J. O. Anthonisz) and councillors (p. 3). The meeting recorded a vote of thanks to retiring officers, particularly R. J. Wilkinson and the Rev. G. M. Reith, and appointed a sub-committee (H. H. Eschke and W. G. St. Clair) to consider proposed alterations to the Society's rules and the quorum requirement for ordinary General Meetings (p. 3).

The adjourned meeting of 4 February 1896 added a proviso to Rule 26 requiring that any amendment proposed by the Committee be stated in the notice summoning the meeting, and resolved unanimously that ladies were eligible for membership, aligning the Straits Branch with the Royal Asiatic Society of Great Britain and Ireland (p. 5).
