# The prehistory of Malaya

This document records the proceedings of the General Meeting of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society, held at the Exchange Rooms on 21 February 1883.

## Summary

The minutes record the attendance of members including Col. S. Dunlop, N. B. Dennys, Hugo Dennys, Bennett Pell, and others, and the approval of six gentlemen provisionally elected by the Council since the previous annual meeting (p. 2–3). The Honorary Secretary read the Annual Report of the Council for 1882, and the Honorary Treasurer read his own report (p. 3).

Officers for 1883 were elected by ballot. The Chairman noted that Mr. W. E. Maxwell, unanimously elected Honorary Secretary, had not yet returned to the Colony, and that Mr. Skinner would continue in the role in the interim (p. 3). The meeting closed with a vote of thanks to the Chairman.
