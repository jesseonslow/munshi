# Mr. R.B. Sharpe on birds collected in Perak

This editorial note accompanies the third and final part of Volume 21 (1948) of the *Journal of the Malayan Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society*, published in October 1948.

## Summary

The editorial announces the contents of the volume, notes that the title-page and index are distributed with this issue, and previews the planned contributors for 1949, including Sir Richard Winstedt, Dato Sir Roland Braddell, Prof. K. A. Nilakanta Sastri, and others (p. 2). It highlights that M. C. ff. Sheppard's paper *A Short History of Trengganu* completes the series of state histories for the Federation of Malaya, and that C. A. Gibson-Hill's paper on Christmas Island completes the series covering Singapore's dependencies. The Society also announces advance information on two forthcoming biographies: one of Captain Francis Light by H. P. Clodd and one of Sir Stamford Raffles by C. E. Wurtzburg (p. 2).

A notice to contributors follows, requesting brevity, avoidance of footnotes, and typed double-spaced manuscripts, and reminding members that the 1949 subscription has increased from $6.00 to $10.00 (p. 2).
