# Raffles, Acheh and the Order of the Golden Sword

This is a brief field note by R. O. Winstedt describing a mock combat ritual observed at Johol, published in the *Journal of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society*, No. 77 (1917), p. 249.

## Summary

Winstedt records his observation of a *bapua* (or *singketa*) ceremony performed before rice planting, in which two parties hurled *puar* rods at one another across a gully to expel evil influences from the fields. He notes the rods were about 3½ feet long, thin, green, and straight, with a flat pared root end, and suggests they may be stems of a ginger-plant (p. 249).

He contextualises the ritual by referencing Blagden's 1896 account in the *J. R. A. S.* and Skeat's *Malay Magic*, and adds that a *pawang* opened the proceedings with an invocation. He further distinguishes *singketa* as a more serious combat using heavier weapons, possibly *batang pisang* (p. 249).
