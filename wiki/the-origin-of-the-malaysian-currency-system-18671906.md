# The origin of the Malaysian currency system (1867–1906

This is the Annual Report for 1903 of the Malaysian Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society, authored by R. Hanitsch and A. Knight, covering the Society's activities, membership, and finances for that year (p. 2).

## Summary

The report notes that the Society's membership stood at 145, with 14 new members elected during the year, and records the deaths of three members including former Secretary A. W. O'Sullivan (p. 2). Only one issue of the Journal (No. 39) was published, with a forthcoming article on Borneo musical instruments by R. Shelford of the Sarawak Museum. The Council reported that no Malay manuscripts deemed worthy of publication had yet been obtained, though two had been offered by Mr. George Maxwell. The Library was rearranged and cataloguing was underway, and the Society noted its twenty-fifth anniversary, having been founded on 4 November 1877 (p. 2).

The Treasurer's account shows opening balances of $3,703.40, receipts of $2,091.67 (principally from subscriptions, journal and map sales, and bank interest), and payments of $1,198.51 (including printing, binding, bookcases, and staff salaries), yielding closing balances of $4,596.56 (p. 3).
