# The Royal Batavia Society of Arts and Letters

This short note by C. B. Kloss records the participation of delegates from British [Malaya](./malaya.md) in the 150th anniversary celebrations of the Koninklijk Bataviaasch Genootschap van Kunsten en Wetenschappen (Royal Batavian Society of Arts and Letters) at [Batavia](./jakarta.md) in April 1928.

## Summary

The note situates the Royal Batavian Society, founded in 1778, as the senior [European](./european.md) learned society in Asia, predating the Asiatic Society of Bengal by six years. It traces the historical connection between the society and British Malaya through [Stamford Raffles](./stamford-raffles.md), who served as its President during his Javanese residence, and notes the recent appointment of [C. O. Blagden](./charles-otto-blagden.md) and [R. O. Winstedt](./richard-olaf-winstedt.md) to Honorary Membership (p. 82).

Captain A. C. Baker, M.C., was appointed by the President and Council of the [Malayan Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society](./malaysian-branch-of-the-royal-asiatic-society.md) to represent the society at the celebrations, where he read a complimentary address from Sir Hayes Marriott and joined excursions to Borobodur and Pramban. C. B. Kloss himself attended as representative of Sir [Hugh Clifford](./sir-hugh-charles-clifford.md), Governor of the [Straits Settlements](./straits-settlements.md), and presented a congratulatory address on behalf of the Scientific Departments of the Governments of British Malaya (p. 83).
