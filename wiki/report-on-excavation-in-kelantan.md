# Report on excavation in Kelantan

This brief geographical note by H. B. Marshall (1918) fixes the position of Gunong Say, a hill in Brunei previously mentioned in connection with James Motley's collections.

## Summary

Marshall situates Gunong Say on the right bank of the river Brunei, approximately 2¼ miles south by west of the town, directly facing the residency on the opposite bank. The hill stands 760 feet high and is accompanied by a neighbouring peak, Gunong Sumur, a mile to the south-east, which appears on Admiralty Charts as "Hamilton Hill." Both are noted as conspicuous landmarks visible to steamers plying between Singapore and Labuan (p. 2).
