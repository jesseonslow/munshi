# A Malay legal digest compiled for ‘Abd al-Ghafur Muhaiyuddin Shah, Sultan of Pahang, 1592–1614 A.D., with undated additions. .E

Editorial note for the third and final part of JMBRAS Volume 21 (1948), published by the Malaysian Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society.

## Summary

This editorial announces that the first part of Volume 22 (1949) will be a miscellaneous number featuring papers by Sir Richard Winstedt, Dato Sir Roland Braddell, Prof. K. A. Nilakanta Sastri, and others (p. 2). It notes that M. C. ff. Sheppard's *A Short History of Trengganu* will complete the series of state histories for the Federation of Malaya, and that C. A. Gibson-Hill's *The Early History of Christmas Island* will complete the series covering Singapore's dependencies (p. 2). Advance notice is given of two forthcoming biographies: one of Captain Francis Light by H. P. Clodd (1948) and one of Sir Stamford Raffles by C. E. Wurtzburg (expected 1949) (p. 2).

The editorial also announces an increase in annual subscriptions from $6.00 to $10.00 for 1949, and includes a notice to contributors requesting brevity, avoidance of footnotes, and specific formatting requirements for manuscripts and line drawings (p. 2).
