# Notes on Malay subjects

"Notes on Malay Subjects" is a brief multi-topic note by R. O. Winstedt, K.B.E., C.M.G., D.Litt., F.R.A., published in JMBRAS Vol. 20, Part I (1947), pp. 140–142.

## Summary

The note addresses four discrete items. First, Winstedt challenges Roland Braddell's identification of the Jawi term *K.l.nggi* in the Kedah Annals with *Kalinga*, arguing from Jawi orthographic conventions that the double *ngg* cannot reduce to *ng* and that no manuscript supports the reading *Kalinga* (p. 140–141). Second, he translates a genealogical list of Shattariya Sufi masters from a Marsden collection manuscript now held at SOAS, which documents the transmission of the order through Java to Trengganu in the eighteenth century, terminating with Lebai Bidin, an Achinese (p. 141–142). Third, he offers a Kelantan manuscript quotation from a recension of the *Hikayat Bakhtiar* as confirmation of Brandstetter's etymology of *pantun* from an Indonesian root *tun* meaning "thread" or "in lines" (p. 142). Fourth, he supplies a Maxwell manuscript note identifying the translator of the *Hikayat Bustamam* and *Hikayat Ganja Mara* as Saudagar Che Puteh, uncle of Penghulu Che Sahid, who translated the works for Mr. Maingy at Kuala Buka (p. 142).
