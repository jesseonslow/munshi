## Biography

Robert Forrest was born in Bury, Lancashire, in 1949. He was educated at Bury Grammar School and Brasenose College, Oxford, where he gained a degree in Maths. Since retiring from a career in teaching that subject in 2011, he has been able to devote more of his time to collecting and studying editions of FitzGerald’s *Rubaiyat of Omar Khayyam*.
