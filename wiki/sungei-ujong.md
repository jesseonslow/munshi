# Sungei Ujong

This is a brief note by W. C. (1900) on the Malay "Susu Rimau" (Tiger's Milk), a vegetable product identified as the sclerotium of *Polyporus sacer*.

## Summary

The author supplements an earlier account (Journal No. 22, p. 340) of Susu Rimau by reporting that he has since obtained fine specimens of the tuber and the fully developed fungus in Singapore and Penang. Mr. Massee of Kew identified the species as *Polyporus sacer*, a widely distributed fungus (p. 101).
