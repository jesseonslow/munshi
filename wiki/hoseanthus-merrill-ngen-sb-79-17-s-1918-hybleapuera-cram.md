# Hoseanthus Merrill, n.gen. SB 79: {17} S 1918 Hybleapuera Cram

This short note by H. N. Ridley protests against Merrill's establishment of the new genus *Hoseanthus* to replace Ridley's own genus *Hosea* (Verbenaceae), arguing that the supposed prior claim by Dennstedt is invalid (p. 17).

## Summary

Ridley contends that Dennstedt's 1818 *Schlüssel zum Hortus Indicus Malabaricus* merely assigned names to poor-quality drawings from Rheede's *Hortus Malabaricus* without any description or even indication of plant order, making them *nomina nuda* of no taxonomic value (p. 17). He argues that substituting *Hoseanthus* for *Hosea* to accommodate an unidentifiable and undescribed name is an unnecessary addition to synonymy that serves only to increase confusion and bulk in the literature.
