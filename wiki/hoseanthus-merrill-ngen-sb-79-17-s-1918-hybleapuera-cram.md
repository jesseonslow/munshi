# Hoseanthus Merrill, n.gen. SB 79: {17} S 1918 Hybleapuera Cram

H. N. Ridley's brief note records the first observation of the moth *Hyblea puera* Cram in the Malay Peninsula, based on specimens collected from mangrove swamps in the Dindings and Province Wellesley in 1897 (p. 104).

Ridley describes how extensive patches of *Avicennia officinalis* (the Malay "Apiapi") near Prai and along the Bruas river had been stripped bare by caterpillars of this species, which he identified through Mr. C. O. Waterhouse from chrysalids collected at Telok Sera (p. 104). The moth, a native of the West Indies, India, Africa, and Java, is described as one inch across the wings with brown upper wings bearing chestnut markings and orange underwings with a waved black bar (p. 105). Ridley notes that the caterpillar feeds exclusively on *Avicennia officinalis*, a valueless tree, and that the Malays reported no prior experience of such devastation (p. 105).
