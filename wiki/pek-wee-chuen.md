## Biography

Pek Wee Chuen holds a PhD in Geography from National Taiwan Normal University and is an assistant professor at the New Era University College of Kajang, Malaysia. His research specializations are historical geography, diasporic Chinese studies, and toponymy. He is the author of *Transformation of the Chinese Society in Johor Bahru, Johor, 1855–1942*.
