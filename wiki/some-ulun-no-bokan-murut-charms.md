# Some ulun-no-bokan (Murut) charms

This short note by H. G. Keith records four specific charms (*tinogum*) of the Ulun-no-Bokan (Murut) people, each with a distinct name and ritual application (p. 330).

## Summary

Keith identifies the generic term for charms in the Bokan dialect as *tinogum* and then lists four specific types. *Susungug* is a charm fed to dogs to improve their hunting ability, administered by boiling a stone in water and pouring the liquid down the dog's throat. *Pananam bilud* is placed with *padi* seed to ensure a crop with fat grains. *Tambar* serves as a preventive against sickness, the charm being soaked in water for bathing. *Panginging dapoh* is used to bring good luck in trading or dowry negotiations, ensuring cheapness when buying and a low dowry when seeking a bride (p. 330).
