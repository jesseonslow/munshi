# The grave-stone of Sultan Shah of Malacca

This short note by Zainal Abidin bin Ahmad offers minor corrections and observations on the decipherment of the grave-stone inscription of Sultan Mansur Shah of Malacca, published in JSBRAS Vol. 86 (1922).

## Summary

The author addresses small but potentially consequential misprints in the spelling of the inscription's reading, including corrections to the rendering of "lis-sultan" and "laisa li'd-dunia" (p. 388). He also considers whether the absence of certain letters (the "n" of a word and the "r" of Mansur) and the lack of diacritical points in the inscription may be attributable to mason errors, letter blending in the interwoven script, or simple wear (p. 388).

The note closes with the author's endorsement of the reconstructions as "really very ingenious" and the reading as "certainly much more acceptable than the one which used to be accepted before it" (p. 388).
