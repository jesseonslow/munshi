# The visit of the ‘King of Brunei’ to the supreme emperor Yung Lo of China: contemporary and ancient accounts

This is a joint obituary notice for the Right Reverend George Frederick Hose, D.D., Bishop of Singapore and Sarawak (1881–1908), written by R. N. Bland and H. N. Ridley and published in the *Journal of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society*, No. 57 (1911), pp. 1–4.

## Summary

Bland's portion (pp. 1–3) traces Hose's career from his 1838 birth and Cambridge education through his ordination in 1863, his chaplaincies in Malacca and Singapore, and his consecration as Bishop in 1881. It notes that his 40 years in the Far East coincided with the Straits Settlements' transition from an Indian dependency to a separate colony, and that his diocese eventually proved too large to manage as a single see, leading to its division. Bland also highlights Hose's founding of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society in November 1877 and his lasting interest in Malay language and Eastern botany (p. 2).

Ridley's portion (pp. 3–4) elaborates on Hose's botanical contributions, particularly his extensive fern collections from Borneo, several species of which bear his name (e.g., *Trichomanes Hosei*, *Davallia Hosei*, *Adiantum Hosei*). It records his introduction of the Para rubber tree to Sarawak, his rediscovery of *Crinum Northianum*, and his role as the Society's first and long-serving President, delivering inaugural and subsequent presidential addresses in 1878–1880 (p. 3).
