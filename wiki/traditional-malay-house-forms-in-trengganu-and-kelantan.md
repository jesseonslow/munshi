# Traditional Malay house forms in Trengganu and Kelantan

This article by Tan Sri Haji [Mubin Sheppard](./mubin-sheppard.md), published in the *[Journal of the Malaysian Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society](./journal-of-the-malaysian-branch-of-the-royal-asiatic-society.md)* (Vol. 42, No. 2, 1969), examines traditional [Malay](./malay.md) house forms in the states of [Trengganu](./terengganu.md) and [Kelantan](./kelantan.md) (pp. 1–9).

## Summary

The document presents Sheppard's study of vernacular domestic [architecture](./architecture.md) in two northeastern Peninsular Malay states, comparing and describing the house typologies characteristic of each region. The article is accompanied by illustrative figures (p. 1).
