# An eye-witness account of the invasion of Java in 1811 — the diary of Lt. W.G.A. Fielding. C. Skinner

This is a brief note by H. N. Ridley on the nesting behaviour of *Draco fimbriatus*, published in the *Journal of the Malaysian Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society* (1905).

## Summary

Ridley records that the bird nests very frequently given its commonness, and that he has often observed it making holes in trees apparently for nesting purposes. On one occasion he knew of an individual that seemed to roost regularly in a hole it had made in a dead bough in the Gardens, where it could be seen every evening sitting in the mouth of the hole. Despite it being the breeding season, he found neither eggs nor young birds in the nest (p. 227).
