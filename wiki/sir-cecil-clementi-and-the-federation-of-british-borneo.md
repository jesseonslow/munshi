# Sir Cecil Clementi and the Federation of British Borneo

This is a brief field note by H. N. Ridley on the nesting behaviour of a common bird species, published in the *Journal of the Malaysian Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society* (1905).

## Summary

Ridley records observations of a bird that nests very frequently due to its commonness, often seen making holes in trees as if to nest. On one occasion he observed an individual that appeared to roost regularly in a hole it had made in a dead bough in the Gardens, visible every evening at the mouth of the hole. Despite it being the breeding season, neither eggs nor young birds were found in the nest (p. 227).
