# Hermanus Neubronner Van Der Tuuk

This is a brief taxonomic note by F. F. Laidlaw describing two species of dragonflies (Odonata) from Sarawak, published in the JSBRAS in 1911.

## Summary

Laidlaw describes a new species, *Orogomphus dyak*, from specimens collected at Mt. Matang and near Kuching (Sarawak Museum), providing detailed morphological measurements, wing venation, and colour descriptions for both male and female (pp. 191–192). He also examines a female specimen tentatively identified as *Orogomphus splendidus* Selgs, noting that while it is distinct from *O. dyak* in size, discoidal triangle shape, anal loop cell count, and labial palp form, it does not fully agree with de Selgs' original description in wing colouring (pp. 192–193).
