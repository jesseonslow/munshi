# Mr. R. Braddell’s ancient times

[R.O. Winstedt](./richard-olaf-winstedt.md)'s brief note is a point-by-point rebuttal of [Roland Braddell](./dato-sir-roland-st-john-braddell.md)'s "Study of Ancient Times in the [Malay](./malay.md) Peninsula" (JMBRAS Vol. XIV, Part III), defending Winstedt's own interpretations in *[A History of Malaya](./a-history-of-malaya.md)* and *[A History of Johore](./a-history-of-johore.md)*.

## Summary

Winstedt challenges Braddell on several specific points: the identification of [Ptolemy](./ptolemy.md)'s River Pelandas with Malay *pelandok* or *melanda*; the proposed link between Iskandar Shah and Skandasisya; the identification of Gangga Negara with Bruas; and the location of the commission for the *[Malay Annals](./malay-annals.md)*, which Winstedt argues was at Pasir Raja on the [Johore](./johor.md) River rather than at [Pasai](./pasai.md) (p. 142). He also corrects Braddell's claim that Lenggiu does not exist, noting it survives as a river name, and explains van der Tuuk's identification of Sang Nila Uttama with Tilottama on the basis of the standard triad of Indra's nymphs (Suprabha, Tilottama, Menaka), arguing that a Malay scribe would naturally have interpolated the third name (p. 143). Winstedt further defends his suggestion that Bichitram Shah represents an interpolation in the *Annals*, pointing to the dual [genealogies](./genealogies.md) in Devic's translation as evidence of textual confusion (pp. 143–144).
