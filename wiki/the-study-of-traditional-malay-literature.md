# The study of traditional Malay literature

This is the Annual Report for 1903 of the Malaysian Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society, authored by R. Hanitsch and A. Knight (p. 2).

## Summary

The report covers the Society's activities and finances for 1903. It notes a membership of 145, lists new members elected during the year, and records the deaths of three members including former Secretary A. W. O'Sullivan (p. 2). The Council reports that only one Journal issue (No. 39) was published, that an article on Borneo musical instruments by R. Shelford was forthcoming, and that two Malay manuscripts offered by George Maxwell were expected for publication (p. 2). The Library was rearranged and cataloguing was underway, and a reciprocal membership arrangement with the Royal Asiatic Society of Bengal was noted (p. 2).

The appended Treasurer's Account shows balances carried forward from 1902 of $3,703.40, receipts of $2,091.67, payments of $1,198.51, and closing balances of $4,596.56 (p. 3).
