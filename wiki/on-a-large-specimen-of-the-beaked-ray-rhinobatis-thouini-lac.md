# On a large specimen of the beaked ray Rhinobatis thouini (Lacep.). Records of the Raffles Museum, No. 24

This short note by N. Smedley describes a large specimen of the beaked ray *Rhinobatis thouini* (Lacep.) held in the Raffles Museum, Singapore.

## Summary

Smedley reports that the specimen agrees closely with Cantor's form except for the absence of a second constriction, a feature also lacking in a smaller cast specimen in the Raffles Museum. He provides measurements of the snout expansion: width at constriction 2½ in., nearer apex 4 in., and length of expanded portion 6 in. (p. 262).

Noting that both Bleeker and Day had treated Cantor's species as a synonym of *thouini*, Smedley concludes that the peculiar expansion of the snout is characteristic of the female of that species (p. 262).
