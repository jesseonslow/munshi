# The bulletin of the Raffles Museum

This is a brief editorial note by C. Boden Kloss announcing the establishment of the *Bulletin of the [Raffles](./stamford-raffles.md) Museum, [Straits Settlements](./straits-settlements.md)* as a third local museum journal in [Malaysia](./malaysia.md), alongside those of the [Federated Malay States](./federated-malay-states.md) [Museums](./museums.md) and the [Sarawak](./sarawak.md) Museum.

## Summary

Kloss notes that Malaysian zoology will occupy less space in the *[Journal of the Malayan Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society](./journal-of-the-malaysian-branch-of-the-royal-asiatic-society.md)* going forward, as the three museum departments now have their own publications covering [anthropology](./anthropology.md), ethnology, [zoology](./zoology.md), [botany](./botany.md), [geology](./geology.md), and related subjects. The Raffles Museum had previously relied on the Society's journal for publication, a gap now filled by its new *Bulletin*. The first number contains a report on the Blattidae of the [Mentawi Islands](./mentawi-islands.md) ("Spolia Mentawiensia") by Dr. R. Hanitsch, formerly Director of the Raffles Museum and Library (p. 84).
