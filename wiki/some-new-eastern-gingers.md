# Some new eastern gingers

This short note by H.N. Ridley describes three new species of eastern gingers (Zingiberaceae) collected from Celebes and the Bismarck Archipelago, published in the *Journal of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society* (1900).

## Summary

Ridley describes *Alpinia (Hellenia) Celebica* n. sp., a herb from Celebes allied to *A. Fraseriana* of Borneo but distinguished by its very deeply cleft lip (p. 98). He then describes *Anomum terminale* n. sp., collected by Micholitz in the Bismarck Archipelago and flowered at the Singapore Botanic Gardens in February 1900; the species is noted for its terminal spike (abnormal among *Anomums*) and red bracts, and Ridley expresses reluctance to erect a separate genus for it (pp. 98–99). Finally, he describes *Tapeinocheilus Koordersiana* n. sp., a tall (25 ft.) plant from Celebes allied to Miquel's *T. pungens* but with larger flowers and pubescent bracts, named in honour of Dr. Koorders for his fine collections in Celebes (p. 99).
