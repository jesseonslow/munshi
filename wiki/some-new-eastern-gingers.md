# Some new eastern gingers

This short note by [H.N. Ridley](./henry-nicholas-ridley.md) describes three new species of eastern gingers (Zingiberaceae) collected from [Celebes](./celebes.md) and the Bismarck Archipelago, published in the *[Journal of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society](./journal-of-the-malaysian-branch-of-the-royal-asiatic-society.md)* (1900).

## Summary

Ridley describes *Alpinia (Hellenia) Celebica* n. sp., a herb from Celebes allied to *A. Fraseriana* of [Borneo](./borneo.md) but distinguished by its very deeply cleft lip (p. 98). He then describes *Anomum terminale* n. sp., collected by Micholitz in the Bismarck Archipelago and flowered at the [Singapore](./singapore.md) Botanic Gardens in February 1900; the species is noted for its terminal spike (abnormal among *Anomums*) and red bracts, and Ridley expresses reluctance to erect a separate genus for it (pp. 98–99). Finally, he describes *Tapeinocheilus Koordersiana* n. sp., a tall (25 ft.) plant from Celebes allied to Miquel's *T. pungens* but with larger [flowers](./flowers.md) and pubescent bracts, named in honour of Dr. Koorders for his fine collections in Celebes (p. 99).
