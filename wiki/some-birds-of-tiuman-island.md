# Some birds of Tiuman Island

This short note by C. Boden Kloss records a list of bird species collected on Tioman Island during two visits in 1899 and 1900, published in the Journal of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society (p. 280).

## Summary

Kloss describes two collecting trips to Tioman Island: a week-long stay at Joara Bay on the east coast in 1899, and a shorter stop at Tingah Bay on the west coast in 1900, both undertaken with Dr. W. L. Abbott. He provides a brief geographical and administrative description of the island, noting its dimensions, elevation (highest peak 3,444 ft.), and its status as a Pahang territory inhabited by a small Malay community (p. 280).

The note records four bird species collected below 500 feet altitude: *Malacopterus magnirostre*, *Stachyris davisoni*, *Cyanoderma erythropterum*, and *Aegithina viridissima*. Kloss notes that no new bird species were obtained and that his original field notes, which would have included additional observed species, were mislaid (p. 280).
