# Bird notes. Records of the Raffles Museum, No. 5

This short note by F. N. Chasen, Keeper of Birds at the Raffles Museum, records several ornithological observations from Singapore and the Malay Peninsula, including measurements of *Zosterops natalis*, records of introduced and escaped cage birds in Singapore, a new mainland record of *Delichon urbica dasypus*, and an assessment of the local status of *Oceanodroma monorhis* at Horsburgh Lighthouse (p. 68).

Chasen documents the establishment of several non-native species in Singapore, most notably *Uroloncha leucogastroides*, a Sumatran-Javan species now found in small flocks across the island, and *Munia oryzivora*, which has spread to neighbouring islets and the Riau Archipelago (p. 69). He also corrects earlier misidentifications of *Sturnia malabarica* and *S. m. nemoricola* as immature *Sturnia turdiformes* (p. 69). The note concludes with a table of measurements for nine specimens of *Oceanodroma monorhis* from the Raffles Museum collection, most obtained at Horsburgh Lighthouse in May 1923 and May 1924 (p. 70).
