# Foreword (J.M. Gullick Festschrift)

This is a one-page foreword by [H.S. Barlow](./henry-sackville-barlow.md), editor of the Festschrift volume honouring [J.M. Gullick](./john-michael-gullick.md), published in [JMBRAS](./journal-of-the-malaysian-branch-of-the-royal-asiatic-society.md) Vol. 72, Part 2 (1999).

## Summary

Barlow reflects on Gullick's half-century of contributions to the Society's Journal and Monograph series, placing his scholarly output alongside that of earlier figures such as [W.E. Maxwell](./sir-william-edward-maxwell.md) and [Sir Richard Winstedt](./richard-olaf-winstedt.md). He highlights Gullick's dual qualities of scholarly rigour and readability, his reliability as a contributor whose manuscripts were "always trouble-free," and his characteristic generosity in sharing knowledge with fellow historians through prompt, detailed correspondence (p. 1). The foreword closes with acknowledgements to the volume's contributors and to Datin Noor Azlina Yunus for her editorial assistance.
