# Bersandui: verses recited by collectors of honey in Rembau

This short note by [O.T. Dussek](./ot-dussek.md) documents a series of [Malay](./malay.md) verses (bersandui) traditionally recited by honey collectors in [Rembau](./rembau.md), published in [JMBRAS](./journal-of-the-malaysian-branch-of-the-royal-asiatic-society.md) Vol. 6, No. 4 (1928).

## Summary

The text presents numbered stanzas (5–8) corresponding to specific stages of the honey-collecting process: burning the tree bud (mëmbakar tunam), cutting the comb (memotong sarang), lowering the comb to the ground (mëngulorkan sarang turun ka-tanah), and the pawang's descent (pawang turun) (pp. 56–57). The verses employ the characteristic Malay pantun form, with nature imagery and metaphorical language—references to birds, [flowers](./flowers.md), and the wind—interwoven with practical descriptions of the activity. A footnote clarifies that "tunam" in the first stanza refers to carrying [fire](./fire.md) (p. 57).
