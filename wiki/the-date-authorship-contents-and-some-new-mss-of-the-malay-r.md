# The date, authorship, contents and some new Mss. of the Malay romance of Alexander the Great

This is a continuation of T. H. Haynes's "English, Sulu, and Malay Vocabulary," originally published in the Journal of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society (1886), here reprinted in the JMBRAS.

## Summary

The document presents a partial alphabetical vocabulary (letters N through Y) comparing English terms with their Sulu and Malay equivalents, with cross-references to Javanese, Sundanese, Batak, Makassarese, Dayak, Tagalog, and Bisayan cognates. Editor's notes (marked "Ed.") provide etymological commentary, including connections to Persian, Sanskrit, Arabic, and Tamil sources. The entries cover a wide range of semantic fields—body parts, actions, natural phenomena, and abstract concepts—and occasionally note dialectal variation or semantic divergence between the languages.
