# Notes on the millipedes, centipedes, scorpions etc., of the Malay Peninsula and Siam

This is the Annual Report for 1901 of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society, authored by M. Hellier, Honorary Treasurer, published in JSBRAS Vol. 37 (1902).

## Summary

The report states that the Society's financial condition remains favourable, lists twelve new members elected since the previous annual meeting, and notes that Journal No. 36 was published during the year with material for No. 37 in the printer's hands (p. 2). The Council expresses regret at the lack of short-note contributions from members despite the Society's size (p. 2).

The report is followed by the Honorary Treasurer's Cash Account for the year ending 31 December 1901, showing total receipts and disbursements of $3,220.90, with the balance carried forward across the Chartered Bank, Mercantile Bank fixed deposit, and cash in hand (p. 3).
