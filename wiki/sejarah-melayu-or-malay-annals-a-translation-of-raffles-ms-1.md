# Sejarah Melayu; or Malay Annals; a translation of Raffles MS 18 {in the Library of the R.A.S., London} C.C. Brown

This document is the Rules of the Straits Asiatic Society, published as front matter in the *Journal of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society*, January 1894 (Vol. 25, pp. xxi–xxiv).

## Summary

The text sets out the constitutional framework of the Straits Asiatic Society, covering membership categories, officers, council composition and duties, meeting procedures, publications, popular lectures, and the amendment process. The Council is defined as comprising the Officers for the current year, with duties including administration of affairs, election of members, selection of papers for the Journal, and library management (p. xxii). The Journal is to be published biannually under Council supervision, with each member entitled to one copy and twenty-four copies placed at the author's disposal (p. xxiii). Amendments to the Rules require written proposal to the Council and a decision at a General Meeting following a committee report (p. xxiv).
