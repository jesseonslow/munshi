# Pre-Islamic Arabia and South-East Asia

This is a brief obituary notice by H. P. Bryson for Lieutenant H. R. Kelham (later Brigadier-General H. R. Kelham, C.B.), published in *JMBRAS* Vol. 29, No. 1 (173), May 1956, pp. 182–183.

## Summary

The notice records Kelham's military service in Perak in 1877 as commander of a detachment of the 74th Highlanders at Kuala Kangsar, and his subsequent publication of narrative accounts in *British Malaya* (1928). It also highlights his ornithological work: his four-part paper in the *Ibis* (1881–2), reprinted in the *JSBRAS* (1882–3), is identified as the first paper devoted to field notes on Malayan birds to appear in a technical journal, and a landmark in local ornithology (p. 183). Kelham died in October 1932, in his 78th year (p. 183).
