# Description of a new species of jungle fowl said to come from Borneo

This is a brief taxonomic description by H. J. Kelsall of a new species of jungle fowl reported to originate from Borneo, published in the *Journal of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society* (1891).

## Summary

The note provides the concluding portion of a plumage description for the type specimen, detailing the rump, tail, underparts, wing, bill, legs, and claws, along with measurements: total length approximately 28 inches, wing 9 inches, bill from gape 1.0 inch, tarsus 3.5 inches (p. 168). Kelsall notes one or two irregular white blotches on the wing quills of the specimen.
