# A list of the ferns of the Malay Peninsula

This document records the proceedings of the Annual General Meeting of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society, held on March 23, 1908, with Dr. Galloway in the Chair.

## Summary

The meeting confirmed the previous year's minutes, accepted the secretary's report and treasurer's account, and passed a resolution expressing the society's sense of loss at the retirement of Bishop Hose, D.D., its founder in 1877 and President for over forty years (p. 2). A letter from Mr. H. C. Robinson was read proposing a systematic study of the fauna of the Malay Peninsula, noting that while reptiles, batrachians, and birds had been well studied, mammals had been little investigated; the society voted a grant of $500 per year for three years to support this work (p. 3). Officers for the ensuing year were elected, with Dr. Galloway as President, H. N. Ridley as Secretary, and R. J. Bartlett as Treasurer (p. 3).
