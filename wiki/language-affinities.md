# Language affinities

This is a continuation of T. H. Haynes' comparative vocabulary of English, Sulu, and Malay, originally published in the Journal of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society (1886), here reproduced in JMBRAS Vol. 16.

## Summary

The text presents alphabetical entries (N through Y) pairing English glosses with Sulu and Malay equivalents, supplemented by cognate forms from Javanese, Sundanese, Batavian, Makassarese, Dayak, Tagalog, and Bisayan. Editorial notes (marked "Ed.") offer etymological observations, such as the possible Sanskrit origin of *astana* (p. 8) and the shared Arabic root *ra'iyyat* behind Turkish *rayah* and Indian *ryot* (p. 9). A further note queries the direction of borrowing for *kapal* between Javanese and Tamil (p. 29). The entries are largely lexical with minimal commentary, serving as a reference tool for comparative Austronesian lexicography.
