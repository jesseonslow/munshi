# A journey to the source of the Indau

The document is the Rules of the Straits Asiatic Society, comprising the front matter (pp. xxi–xxiv) of *Journal of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society*, Vol. 25 (January 1894). It is an institutional governance text rather than a scholarly article.

## Summary

The Rules set out the constitutional framework of the Straits Asiatic Society across eight sections: membership (including provisions for Honorary Members proposed by the Council), officers (a President, two Vice-Presidents—one resident in Penang—an Honorary Secretary and Librarian, an Honorary Treasurer, and five Councillors), the Council's duties and quorum requirements, meeting procedures (Annual General Meeting in January, monthly General Meetings, quorum of eleven), publications (a semi-annual Journal under Council supervision, with distribution and reprint provisions), popular lectures, and the amendment process (p. xxi–xxiv). The document reflects the Society's dual-centre structure between Penang and Singapore and its function as a learned society publishing a journal, maintaining a library, and hosting paper readings.
