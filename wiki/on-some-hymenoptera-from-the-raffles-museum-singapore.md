# On some Hymenoptera from the Raffles Museum, Singapore

This is a short taxonomic note by P. Cameron listing undetermined Hymenoptera sent to him by Dr. Hanitsch from the Raffles Museum, Singapore, as a contribution to the knowledge of the island's hymenopterous fauna (p. 119).

## Summary

The note provides a list of species identified from the collection, including several previously recorded taxa such as *Evania appendigaster*, *Discolia decorata*, *Vespa cincta*, and *Anthophora zonata*, with brief remarks on their distribution and taxonomic relationships (pp. 119–123). Three new species are described: *Piageta ruficollis*, a black and red spider wasp of nearly 10 mm length, compared to *P. rufivenis* (p. 119); *Icaria singapurensis*, a brownish-black and yellow wasp of 14 mm, compared to *I. 4-maculata* (p. 120); and *Icaria rufinoda*, a deep black wasp with white pubescence and a ferruginous petiole of 12 mm, compared to *I. lugubris* (p. 121). A new species of *Bracon*, *B. spilogaster*, is also described from Ternate (p. 123).
