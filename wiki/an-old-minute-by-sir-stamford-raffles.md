# An old minute by Sir Stamford Raffles

This document records the proceedings of the Annual General Meeting of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society, held at the Raffles Museum on 28 January 1892.

## Summary

The minutes record the unanimous adoption of a resolution expressing the sentiments of the whole Society, proposed by Mr. Justice Goldney and seconded by Mr. Kynersley (p. 3). The Secretary's and Treasurer's reports were read and adopted, and a motion by the Hon'ble J. W. Bonser, seconded by Mr. H. T. Haughton, called for the new Council to revise and correctly print the Society's Rules (p. 3).

Officers elected for the ensuing year included Major-General Sir Charles Warren as President, Bishop G. F. Hose (Singapore) and D. Logan (Penang) as Vice-Presidents, H. N. Ridley as Honorary Secretary, H. T. Haughton as Honorary Treasurer, and five Councillors (p. 3). The meeting closed with a unanimous vote of thanks to the Chairman.
