# Notes on a trip to Gunong Benom in Pahang

This is the Annual Report for 1903 of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society, authored by R. Hanitsch and A. Knight, published in JSBRAS Vol. 39 (1903).

## Summary

The report states that the Society's financial position remains satisfactory, with total balances carried forward of $4,596.56 (p. 3). Membership stood at 145, with fourteen new members elected during the year, and the Council recorded the deaths of former Secretary A. W. O'Sullivan, D. H. Wise, and James Driver (p. 2).

Only one Journal number (No. 39) was published in 1903, with No. 40 expected shortly; an article by R. Shelford on Borneo musical instruments was noted as forthcoming (p. 2). The Council reported that no Malay manuscripts deemed worthy of publication had yet been obtained, though two had been offered by Mr. George Maxwell. The Library was rearranged and cataloguing was underway. The report also notes a reciprocal membership arrangement with the Royal Asiatic Society of Bengal and marks the Society's twenty-fifth anniversary, having been founded on 4 November 1877 (p. 2).
