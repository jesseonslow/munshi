# A new Balanophera from Tenimber Islands

This short note by H. N. Ridley describes a new species of *Balanophora* (*B. Micholitzii*) collected from the Tenimber Islands, published in the Journal of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society in 1903.

## Summary

Ridley notes that the Tenimber Islands had been botanically neglected since H. O. Forbes's 1882 visit, when his *Balanophora* specimens were lost in a fire. The new material was collected by Mr. Micholitz during a recent visit and preserved in formaline, which Ridley commends as superior to spirit for retaining the natural colour of these fleshy plants (p. 207).

The species is described with a small rounded rhizome, two or three stems bearing about eight orbicular white leaves, and a globose yellowish bisexual capitulum. Male flowers are arranged in two or three whorls with four oblong fleshy sepals and horse-shoe-shaped anthers; female flowers are obconic and clavate with numerous spadicles. Ridley places it as nearly allied to *B. Zollingeri* Fawcett from Salayer, south of Celebes, but notes it is much larger in all parts and has more distinctly club-shaped female flowers with longer stalks bearing well-developed spadicles (p. 207).
