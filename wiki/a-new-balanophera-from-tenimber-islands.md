# A new Balanophera from Tenimber Islands

This short note by [H. N. Ridley](./henry-nicholas-ridley.md) describes a new species of *Balanophora* (*B. Micholitzii*) collected from the Tenimber [Islands](./islands.md), published in the [Journal of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society](./journal-of-the-malaysian-branch-of-the-royal-asiatic-society.md) in 1903.

## Summary

Ridley notes that the Tenimber [Islands](./islands.md) had been botanically neglected since H. O. Forbes's 1882 visit, when his *Balanophora* specimens were lost in a [fire](./fire.md). The new material was collected by Mr. Micholitz during a recent visit and preserved in formaline, which Ridley commends as superior to spirit for retaining the natural colour of these fleshy [plants](./plants.md) (p. 207).

The species is described with a small rounded rhizome, two or three stems bearing about eight orbicular white leaves, and a globose yellowish bisexual capitulum. Male [flowers](./flowers.md) are arranged in two or three whorls with four oblong fleshy sepals and horse-shoe-shaped anthers; female flowers are obconic and clavate with numerous spadicles. Ridley places it as nearly allied to *B. Zollingeri* Fawcett from Salayer, south of [Celebes](./celebes.md), but notes it is much larger in all parts and has more distinctly club-shaped female [flowers](./flowers.md) with longer stalks bearing well-developed spadicles (p. 207).
