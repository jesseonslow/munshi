# Three new races of Sciurus vittatus. F.N. Chasen and C.B. Kloss. Records of the Raffles Museum, No. 1

This brief note by F. N. Chasen and C. B. Kloss, published as Records of the Raffles Museum No. 1, describes three new subspecies of the red-bellied squirrel (*Sciurus vittatus*) from islands in the Malay Peninsula and the Rhio Archipelago (p. 57).

The authors first outline the distribution of the two major subspecies groups—*miniatus* (continental, with rufous tail-tips) and *vittatus* (island and southern forms, with annulated tail-tips)—before describing the three new races. *S. v. lighti* is from Penang Island and is distinguished from mainland *miniatus* by a less extensive rufous tail area and a dark lateral stripe that is shorter, less black, and grizzled with tawny (p. 58). *S. v. stellaris* is from Bintang Island, Rhio Archipelago, and is a *peninsularis*-group form with a notably darker, more blackened back (p. 58). *S. v. lunaris* is from Bulan Island, Rhio Archipelago, and differs from *stellaris* in having slightly less rufous underparts, greyer facial sides, a creamy (rather than buff) pale lateral stripe, and a paler tail (p. 58).
