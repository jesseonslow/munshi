# Notes on ancient times in Malaya. Parts 2 and 3

This index compiles all papers published in the Journals of the Malayan Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society from Volume 1 (1923) through Volume 20 (1947), encompassing the full pre-war series and the first post-war volume. It is organised in two parts: a chronological author index providing full titles and references, and a subject index covering topics, places, and biological taxa. The index was compiled by C. A. Gibson-Hill and published in 1948 (p. 1).

## Summary

**Scope.** Covers all twenty volumes of the Journal (1923–1947), comprising approximately fifty-five parts. The subject index encompasses disciplines including archaeology and prehistory, botany, zoology (birds, mammals, reptiles, fishes, insects, invertebrates), ethnology, linguistics, history, literature, law, geology, and material culture, as well as place-based entries for states and administrative areas in Malaya and adjacent territories (pp. 1, 37).

**Organization.** The index is divided into two principal parts. Part 1 arranges papers chronologically under author names, giving full titles and complete references. Part 2 (the subject index) lists papers under natural group headings, using condensed references (author and year only) that cross-reference back to Part 1 for full details (p. 1). The subject index employs a hierarchical scheme with major headings (e.g., "Archaeology & Prehistory", "Botany", "Birds") and sub-headings for specific topics or localities (pp. 37–58). Two preliminary lists precede the index proper: one recording publication dates and stocks held for each part of Volumes 1–20, and another listing the destinations of copies distributed as donations or exchanges to public institutions worldwide (pp. 2–8).

**Usage.** Researchers seeking a specific paper by author should consult Part 1; those searching by topic, place, or taxon should use Part 2. The publication dates and stocks list (pp. 2–3) assists in locating available back numbers, while the exchanges and donations list (pp. 4–8) identifies public institutions holding complete or partial sets of the Journal, with asterisks denoting those confirmed as available for consultation on their premises (pp. 2, 4).

## Context

The index explicitly excludes the earlier Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society (Nos. 1–86, July 1878 to November 1922), for which a separate index was compiled by C. E. Wurtzburg and published in 1927 (*J.M.B.R.A.S.*, Vol. 5, pt 4) (p. 1). Appendix 7 of the present volume provides a list of the Straits Branch Journals with publication dates and stocks held (pp. 64–66). The index notes that the entire stock of Vol. 5, pt 3 (1927), containing the Jawi (Arabic-script Malay) text of the *Tale of Trong Pipit*, was removed by Japanese authorities during the occupation and has not been recovered (p. 2). Title-pages for Volumes 1–5, 7–12, and 14–16 were also lost during the occupation, having been used as supports for drying seeds in botanical experiments (p. 2).
