# The Moluccan spice monopoly, 1770–1824. H.R.C. Wright. Passages concerning Bencoolen written in collaboration with J. Bastin

This is a botanical article by A. L. Butler describing newly identified species of Eastern gingers (Zingiberaceae), published in the Journal of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society, July 1900, No. 34.

## Summary

The text presents formal descriptions of three new species: *Alpinia Celebica* n. sp., a tall herb from Celebes with a deeply cleft lip allied to *A. Fraseriana* of Borneo; *Anomum terminale* n. sp., from the Bismarck Archipelago (Micholitz collection), notable for its terminal spike and red bracts, which Butler found difficult to refer to any existing genus; and *Tapeinocheilus Koordersiana* n. sp., a 25-foot-tall plant from Celebes allied to Miquel's *T. pungens*, named in honour of Dr. Koorders (p. 3–4). The opening fragment also contains a brief zoological note on a flying frog specimen (*Rhacophorus*), comparing its measurements and colouration with Bornean and Perak specimens (p. 2).
