# The Colonial Office and the Malay States, 1867–73

This is the Annual Report for 1901 of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society, authored by M. Hellier, Honorary Treasurer.

## Summary

The report states that the Society's financial condition remains favourable, lists twelve new members elected since the previous annual meeting, and notes that one journal (No. 36) was published during the year with material for No. 37 in the printer's hands (p. 2). The Council expresses regret at the lack of contributions from members, particularly short notes on features and occurrences of interest in the Malay Peninsula (p. 2).

The report is followed by the Honorary Treasurer's Cash Account for the year ending 31 December 1901, showing total receipts and payments of $3,220.90, with the largest expenditure being the discount on foreign notes ($1,053.41) and the largest income source being the fixed deposit at the Mercantile Bank ($1,035.30) (p. 3).
