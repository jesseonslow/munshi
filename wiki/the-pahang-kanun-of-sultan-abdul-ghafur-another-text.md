# The “Pahang Kanun” of Sultan ‘Abdul Ghafur: another text

[R.W. Jakeman](./rw-jakeman.md) presents an additional text of the Pahang Kanun, the legal code associated with Sultan 'Abdul Ghafur of [Pahang](./pahang.md), in a brief note published in the *[Journal of the Malayan Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society](./journal-of-the-malaysian-branch-of-the-royal-asiatic-society.md)* (1951).

## Summary

This short note (pp. 150–151) documents another version or manuscript of the Pahang Kanun, the Malay legal code attributed to Sultan 'Abdul Ghafur. The title's emphasis on "another text" indicates that Jakeman is contributing an additional textual witness to the corpus of Pahang legal [literature](./literature.md), likely for comparative or philological purposes. The note is consistent with the broader scholarly effort in mid-20th-century Malayan studies to collect, collate, and publish Malay legal manuscripts from the [Malay](./malay.md) states.
