# Elephant terms in Perak

This short note by J. I. Miller, M.C.S., documents a set of elephant-handling commands used by Gembalas in the Kuala Kangsar and upper Perak districts, possibly of Patani origin (p. 364).

## Summary

Miller lists fifteen terms alongside their Malay equivalents and meanings, covering basic commands such as "come here," "lie down," "walk," directional cues, and specific handling instructions like trampling brushwood or dipping the head in water (pp. 364–365). The note also references the common equipment terms *kusa* (goad), *rengka* (saddle), and *singkla* (shackle), and cross-references an earlier entry in the *Journal of the Straits Branch of the R.A.S.* (1885, p. 32).
